ART OF IMITATION MIXTAPE

soundcloud spotify youtube

(electronicore, trance, dubstep)

1. slenderman vs jeff the killer

slenderman vs jeff the killer produced by me, this entire project is produced and mixed by me to avoid redundancy. the opening sound sounded similar to the slender game menu, and i was thinking of slenderman vs jeff the killer all day so i said fuck it that should be the name. the initial goal was to make something similar to Asking Alexandria's "Alerion" without biting too hard. very happy with this. (Recorded in London, UK)

2. seen too far

seen too far, i was listening to gunslikegirls and on "Prices Switch" there was 20 second breakdown towards the end that sounded excellent and it pissed me off that they didn't make that the entire song. Hence, seen too far was born. although, i didn't use the riff for the entire song either since i am always itching to add electronic elements. as any electronicore artist would...(Recorded in Brooklyn, New York)

3. so last march

so last march, i was listening to "Stolen Wings" by a soundcloud artist named valentine. and i was thinking wouldnt this be extra awesome if done in a more upbeat way. so i came up with something so upbeat to the point where you wouldn't recognize it even if played side by side. fun fact: i created the trance section as an open for my boy Monke Gum, but he did not have time to do his verse. so i just left it as is with only my background scream in the beginning. (Recorded in London, UK)

4. spread across the floor

spread across the floor, if you've been reading these in order, you'll notice a reoccuring theme where there's a main source of inspiration. but then the song completely transforms into something else. i guess that might be art creation in general. anyway, this song is no exception i was listening to Lust Bug by Skrillex and i was like fuck, the production is so well done and intertwined with his flow. thats the main inspo for the dubstep wubs coming in pre chorus and the tempo of this song. i enjoy songs more when they feel like the performer and instrumental are strung together. the common process today is just hop on a beat n catch a flow. shits cool but often times you'll hear similar flows and production amongst a scene *cough**cough* the ug rap scene atm. its not as fun for me and thats why i stopped rapping entirely. (Recorded in London, UK)

5. stick to fiction

stick to fiction, the earliest non-single song i had in the works. with just the first minute of the beat made on my old laptop soon after seen too far was done. the opening was inspired by I See Star's "End of the World Party" opening. but i needed something digestible to non-HxC/MxC fans so i soon made the main beat trance and it ended up being catchy af.(Recorded in London, UK)

6. seize the only

seize the only, the main inspo behind this one was Emmure's "Chicago's Finest". there was bound to be one slow song on here and i needed to make a sibling for those who liked seen too far. (Recorded in Brooklyn, New York)

7. smile dog

smile dog, the main breakdown came about when i realized i hadn't really varied the types of synths i'd been using. so i aimed for something different and managed to come across some shit that sounded straight from undertale. the tempo structure was inspired by BMTH's "Chelsea Smile" and "The Comedown". and ofc the title was just another creepypasta addition to the tape's intro (Recorded in London, UK)

8. take hide forget

take hide forget, really was just messing around on the keyboard at first. the least imitating song this project. i mean the mixtape's name was already half ironic since the end results stray away from their initial inspiration. so that's why i had no issue including this song here.

9. epic316

epic316, i was really just in the shower and the beginning popped into my head i kept humming it til i could get it down on a voice note. the only reason this song isn't considered the least imitating song like #8 is because the ending transition is inpsired by Asking Alexandria's "Hiatus".

10. i was once possibly maybe a monkey king

i was once possibly maybe a monkey king, the title is directly taking from Asking Alexandria's "I Was Once, Possibly, Maybe a Cowboy King". However, sonically the intro is directly isnpired by "If You Can't Ride Two Horses At Once..." This song is a major breaktrhough in my catalog since it is 5 minutes. I was tired of 2 minutes, and I've tried to make all the songs above 2 minutes. I'm done making ringtones for real. With the end goal of reaching 5 minutes I decided to just put in different feels to the same song without taking long ambient shortcuts to fill time. Hence the breakdown of thirds into dubstep then back to a simple poppy vibe. I would love for 4 and 5 minutes to be the default moving forward but sometimes short and sweet does have its advantages.

if you've read this far thank you, i appreciate anyone who gives this tape a listen. it may be my best yet
